## Блоки для вставки (дословно в начало каждого промта)

```text
STYLE LOCK — "Academy of Numbers"
Art style: cinematic painterly anime-style illustration, semi-realistic, high production value. Rich glossy rendering, dramatic volumetric lighting, warm golden bloom and glow. Expressive large eyes, appealing stylized character art. NOT flat vector, NOT 3D render, NOT photorealistic.
Palette (strict): deep navy / midnight indigo background, warm glowing amber-gold as the single dominant accent, small cool teal highlights, cream-white for cloth. Gold marks what matters.
Signature frame: an ornate golden filigree border with corner flourishes framing the whole image, like an illuminated storybook page. Present in every image.
Signature motifs: glowing golden orbs of light; ribbons of golden light curling through the air; drifting gold sparks; a brass magnifying-glass pendant.
Mood: fairytale detective mystery inside a magical athletic academy at night. Warm, wondrous, inviting — never grim.
Age-appropriate: young teenage student, modest athletic clothing, no sexualization.
Avoid: any existing franchise character or logo; watermarks; letters, words, numbers or written text of any kind; distorted lettering; gore; money or dollar signs; flat vector look; muddy noir.
```
