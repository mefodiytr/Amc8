# Модуль 1 · Урок 1 · Промты картинок
> Визуальный мир: детективное агентство · Реквизит: доска улик, лупа, карточки-подозреваемые, красные нити

## Кадр 1 — Задача
**Назначение:** крючок, слайд 1 (большое число под лупой).
**Промт (EN, для GPT Image):**
> A detective agency desk scene, a large magnifying glass hovering over a suspect card showing
> a big multi-digit number, cork evidence board with red strings in the background, warm
> illustrative vector style, clean and friendly but not cartoonish. Only numerals and symbols
> on the card, no text labels, no words. Free space at the top reserved for a title.

## Кадр 2 — Подсказка
**Назначение:** приём «две улики», слайд 3 (число и две проверки).
**Промт (EN):**
> Same detective agency style and props. A suspect card with a number connected by two red
> strings to two small evidence tags — one tag shows a "÷2" check mark, the other a "÷3" check
> mark. Magnifying glass nearby. Only numerals and the symbols ÷, +, =, ✓; no text labels, no
> words. Warm illustrative vector style. Free space at the top reserved for a title.

## Кадр 3 — Решение
**Назначение:** раскрытое дело, слайд-босс (делимость на 36 = 4·9).
**Промт (EN):**
> Same detective agency style. A "case closed" board: one central number card linked by red
> strings to two clue tags labeled with numerals "4" and "9", a small "36" badge in the corner,
> magnifying glass resting on the desk. Only numerals and symbols (×, =), no text labels, no
> words. Warm illustrative vector style. Free space at the top reserved for a title.
